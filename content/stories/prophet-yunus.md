## A prophet whose people would not listen

Allah sent Yūnus ﷺ to a great city to call his people to worship Allah
alone. He called them for a long time, and they would not listen. At last he
left them, angry and tired, but he left before Allah had told him to go.

## The ship and the sea

He came to a ship and boarded it, and the ship set out. Then the sea grew
rough and the ship grew heavy. The people on board drew lots to decide who
should go overboard, and the lot fell on Yūnus ﷺ. He was cast into the sea,
and a great fish swallowed him.

## Three darknesses

Now he was in the dark of the fish, inside the dark of the deep sea, inside
the dark of the night. He could not see, and nobody could reach him. But
Allah could hear him. And in that darkness Yūnus ﷺ called out:

> There is no god except You. Glory be to You. I have certainly been among
> the wrongdoers.
> - Sūrat al-Anbiyāʾ, 21:87

He did not ask for anything. He praised Allah, and he admitted what he had
done.

## The answer

Allah answered him. He was brought out onto an open shore, weak and unwell,
and Allah caused a plant to grow over him to shade him. Then Allah sent him
back to his people, and this time they believed, all of them, and Allah
let them enjoy their lives for a while.

## What to take from it

Yūnus ﷺ was a prophet, and still he was corrected. Being corrected is not
the end of anything. And there is no place so dark, so deep, or so far away
that Allah cannot hear you in it. If the sea and the night and the fish
were not too much, then nothing you are inside of is too much either.
